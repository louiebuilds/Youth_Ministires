# v0.11.0 — Milestone 10 Curriculum & Lessons

**Released:** 2026-07-30

## Added

- Ministry-only searchable lesson library
- Teaching objectives, scripture references, teaching notes, preparation notes,
  and discussion guides
- Curriculum plans with ordered lesson sequences
- Published volunteer read-only experience
- HTTPS video and teaching-resource links
- Private PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, and MP4 uploads
- Short-lived protected downloads and archive retention

## Security and Privacy

- Ministry managers maintain curriculum; volunteers read published content.
- Family accounts are denied by navigation, route, database, and storage.
- Private storage enforces approved types and size limits.
- Generated paths avoid participant names.
- Material changes and download authorization are audited.
- Identifiable photos or videos of minors remain prohibited.
