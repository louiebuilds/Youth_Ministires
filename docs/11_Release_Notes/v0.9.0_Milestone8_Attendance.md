# v0.9.0 — Milestone 8 Attendance

**Released:** 2026-07-27

## Added

- Manual and class attendance sessions
- Searchable student rosters and attendance corrections
- Finalized attendance records
- Student check-in and authorized-pickup check-out
- Short-lived one-use family QR passes
- Care-alert review and emergency rosters
- Temporary visitor check-in and check-out
- Audited accidental check-in correction and safe re-entry
- Date-filtered attendance and event check-in summaries
- Existing-household-adult relationship creation with explicit permissions

## Security and Privacy

- Ministry roles and event assignments control attendance and custody access.
- Families can identify only their accessible household through a one-use pass.
- QR passes do not authorize custody actions.
- Medical details appear only in the protected operational workspace.
- Pickup authorization is child-specific and never inferred from household
  membership.
- Direct attendance, custody, visitor, token, and relationship mutations are
  restricted in favor of validated and audited functions.
