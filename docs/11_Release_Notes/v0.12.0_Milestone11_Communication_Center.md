# v0.12.0 — Milestone 11 Communication Center

**Released:** 2026-08-03

## Added

- Audience-scoped announcements with publication, search, and archiving
- Reusable in-app, email, and SMS templates
- Parent and volunteer recipient previews with masked destinations
- Synthetic in-app, email, and SMS delivery records
- Communication history and personal notification read-state controls

## Security and Privacy

- Ministry managers control announcements, templates, and composition.
- Parents and volunteers see only authorized announcements and their own
  notifications.
- Recipient preferences suppress unauthorized channels.
- Email and SMS delivery is synthetic and contacts no external provider.
- Communication actions retain audit and history evidence.

## Verification

Synthetic tests, earlier regressions, ESLint, TypeScript, the production build,
connected Supabase checks, and administrator/family acceptance passed,
including **Mark as read** and **Mark all as read**.

## Deferred

Real email/SMS providers, scheduled and event-triggered communication, and
native browser/mobile push are deferred. Milestone 12 is not included.
