# v0.16.0 — Milestone 16 Reporting & Analytics

**Released:** August 11, 2026

## Added

- Unified manager Reporting workspace
- Live Attendance, check-in, Event, Volunteer/Scheduling, Growth, and Ministry
  Health projections
- Private saved-report configuration lifecycle
- CSV and Excel exports plus print-friendly reporting
- Protected reporting RPCs, export auditing, and live Dashboard summary

## Security

Reporting requires `reports.view` and an active Platform Administrator, Youth
Pastor, or Staff Member account. Volunteer, parent, and anonymous ministry-wide
access is denied. Saved reports are creator-private, direct tables remain
deny-by-default, and restricted data is excluded.

## Acceptance Correction

Corrected custom ranges ignored when the form also sent `preset=30`. Explicit
validated dates now take precedence and drive every projection and export.
Invalid ranges are visible, trend labels remain within the window, and the
August 3–9 regression excludes July 26 data.

## Accepted and Deferred

Product Owner live acceptance passed across all report domains, saved reports,
exports, print output, and authorization boundaries. A possible `N/A` label for
zero-position schedules and seven production dependency advisories (five high,
two moderate) remain for future UX/Production Readiness work. No forced or
breaking upgrade was performed. Milestone 15 remains paused, and Milestone 17
requires separate approval.
