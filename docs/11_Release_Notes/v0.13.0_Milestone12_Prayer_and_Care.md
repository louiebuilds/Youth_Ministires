# v0.13.0 — Milestone 12 Prayer & Care

**Released:** 2026-08-04

## Added

- Sanitized signed-in public prayer list
- Leadership and assignment-scoped private prayer workflows
- Answered-prayer and archive lifecycle with retained history
- Confidential care notes and hospital-visit records
- Assigned care follow-ups with completion, cancellation, and history
- Confidential-note archival
- FUMC Youth Ministries login branding

## Security and Privacy

- Families and volunteers receive no prayer identity or confidential details.
- Care notes and hospital visits are limited to authorized leadership and
  explicitly assigned staff.
- Direct table and private audit-helper access remain denied.
- Confidential content is excluded from communication and audit metadata.

## Verification

Synthetic database and authorization tests, all earlier milestone regressions,
ESLint, TypeScript, the production build, connected Supabase checks, and
administrator/family acceptance passed.
