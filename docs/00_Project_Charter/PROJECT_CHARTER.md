# Project Charter
## Youth Ministries Platform

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Project Status:** Approved  
**Project Phase:** Milestone 0 – Planning & Project Foundation

---

# Executive Summary

The Youth Ministries Platform is a secure, web-based ministry management system designed to simplify the administration of youth ministries while strengthening communication between ministry leaders, volunteers, and families.

Version 1.0 focuses exclusively on Youth Ministry. Although the platform will be architected to support future expansion, additional ministry areas are outside the scope of this release.

The platform will provide a public-facing ministry website together with a secure management portal for parents, volunteers, ministry staff, the Youth Pastor, and the Product Owner/System Administrator.

---

# Project Vision

Provide a modern, secure, and easy-to-use platform that allows youth ministry leaders to spend less time managing administrative tasks and more time investing in students and families.

---

# Project Mission

Provide churches with a secure, intuitive youth ministry platform that strengthens communication, simplifies administration, protects students, equips volunteers, and gives ministry leaders more time for ministry.

---

# Project Motto

> Less administration. More ministry.

---

# North Star

Give ministry leaders more time to minister.

---

# Problem Statement

Many youth ministries rely on multiple disconnected tools, paper forms, spreadsheets, email chains, and text messages to manage ministry operations.

This often results in:

- Duplicate data entry
- Poor communication
- Difficult attendance tracking
- Paper permission forms
- Volunteer confusion
- Administrative overhead
- Increased risk of errors
- Difficulty maintaining student privacy

The Youth Ministries Platform will centralize these processes into a single secure system.

---

# Privacy Commitment

Student privacy is a foundational requirement.

Version 1.0 will not support:

- Public student directories
- Public student information
- Storage of identifiable photos of minors
- Public galleries of minors

Access to sensitive information will be controlled through role-based permissions and audit logging.

# Development Methodology

Development follows a milestone-based workflow.

For every milestone:

1. Define the milestone scope.
2. Complete the work.
3. Verify functionality.
4. Correct issues.
5. Update documentation.
6. Obtain approval.
7. Close the milestone.

Recommendations and future enhancements are discussed only after a milestone has been completed and approved.

---

# Quality Standards

Each milestone must include verification of:

- Build success
- TypeScript correctness
- Lint status
- Feature validation
- Navigation
- Security
- Database functionality
- Documentation updates
