# Prayer & Care User Guide

## Ministry administrators and youth pastors

Open **Prayer & Care** to create public, leadership, or private prayer requests.
Use **Mark answered** to record an answer and **Archive** when the request no
longer belongs in the active list. Answer information remains in retained
history after archival.

Use **Confidential care notes & hospital visits** only for authorized pastoral
care information. Active notes can be archived. Use **Care follow-ups** to
assign a caregiver, due date, priority, and instructions. Complete or cancel an
active follow-up; finished items appear in read-only history.

## Families and volunteers

The Prayer List shows only sanitized summaries explicitly marked public. It
does not reveal the person's identity, request details, assignments, care
notes, hospital visits, or follow-ups.

## Safety boundary

Use synthetic information during development. Never copy confidential prayer
or care details into announcements, email, SMS, or ordinary audit notes. Access
does not authorize disclosure outside the pastoral-care team.
