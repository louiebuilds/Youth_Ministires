# Prayer & Care User Guide

## Ministry administrators and youth pastors

Open **Prayer & Care** and use the focused **Overview**, **Prayer Requests**,
**Care & Visits**, **Follow-ups**, and **Archived** sections. Creation forms stay
closed until you select the corresponding New action.

Create prayer requests as **Public signed-in summary**, **Ministry leadership**,
or **Private oversight**. Request cards display the stored visibility using
these readable labels.

For an active request, open **Manage request** and choose **Edit request** to
correct its Person, Category, Visibility, Title, or Details. Save the changes,
or choose **Cancel** to return to the unchanged request card. Use **Mark
answered** separately to record an answer and **Archive** when the request no
longer belongs in the active list. Answer information remains in retained
history after archival.

Use **Care & Visits** only for authorized pastoral care information. Hospital
visits use the Hospital care category rather than a separate subsystem.
Confidential narratives remain hidden until intentionally opened. Active notes
can be archived; retained confidential narratives remain protected in
**Archived**.

Use **Follow-ups** to assign a caregiver, due date, priority, and instructions.
Instructions remain hidden until intentionally opened. Complete or cancel an
active follow-up; finished items appear in retained read-only history.

## Families and volunteers

The Prayer List shows only sanitized summaries explicitly marked public. It
does not reveal the person's identity, request details, assignments, care
notes, hospital visits, or follow-ups.

## Safety boundary

Use synthetic information during development. Never copy confidential prayer
or care details into announcements, email, SMS, or ordinary audit notes. Access
does not authorize disclosure outside the pastoral-care team.
