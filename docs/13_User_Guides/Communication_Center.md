# Communication Center User Guide

## Ministry managers

Open **Communications** to create, edit, publish, search, and archive
announcements. Select **Manage templates** to maintain reusable in-app, email,
and SMS content.

Select **Compose test message** to choose parents or volunteers and preview the
eligible audience. Email addresses and phone numbers are masked. Recipients
without an authorized channel are shown as suppressed.

All Milestone 11 email and SMS actions are synthetic. Selecting **Complete
synthetic delivery** records delivery history and audit evidence but does not
contact an external provider or send a real message.

## Parents and volunteers

Open **Communications** to view announcements for your audience and personal
in-app notifications. Unread notifications are highlighted. Use **Mark as
read** for one notification or **Mark all as read** for the full list. Read
notifications remain available as history.

Parents and volunteers cannot compose messages, manage templates, or access
another account's notifications.

## Safety boundary

Do not enter real confidential student, medical, counseling, prayer, or care
details in general communications. Use synthetic content during development.
Real email, SMS, scheduled delivery, event-triggered automation, and native
mobile/browser push require later approval.
