# Curriculum and Lessons User Guide

## Ministry Managers

Open **Curriculum** to search lessons and curriculum plans.

- Use **Create lesson** to record teaching objectives, scripture references,
  teaching notes, discussion questions, preparation notes, and audience.
- Publish a lesson when it is ready for volunteer use.
- Use **Create plan** to organize lessons into an ordered curriculum.
- Add HTTPS video or teaching links from a lesson workspace.
- Upload only approved synthetic or ministry teaching files: PDF, DOCX, PPTX,
  TXT, or MP4. Never upload identifiable media of minors.
- Archive outdated plans, lessons, or resources instead of deleting history.

## Volunteers

Volunteers can open **Curriculum** and read published plans, lessons,
discussion guides, and teaching resources. Volunteers cannot create, edit,
reorder, upload, or archive content.

## Families

Curriculum is ministry-only. Parent or Guardian accounts do not receive the
Curriculum navigation item and cannot open curriculum routes or files.

## Private Downloads

Private-file download links are short-lived. If a link expires, return to the
lesson and select the download again.
